# Standard Operating Procedure: Incident Escalation & Contingency Rerouting

## 1. Purpose
Define the escalation protocol when a temperature excursion or reefer breakdown occurs during transit.

## 2. Severity Levels
- **Level 1 (Notice):** Temperature variance within ±1.5°C for under 30 minutes. Action: Re-check telemetry after 15 minutes.
- **Level 2 (Warning):** Temperature variance exceeding threshold for 30–60 minutes. Action: Alert driver to inspect reefer power and seals.
- **Level 3 (Critical Alert):** Excursion lasting > 60 minutes or exceeding safety limits by > 4°C. Action: Initiate emergency reroute to nearest cold-storage facility.

## 3. Emergency Rerouting Guidelines
- Search the route corridor for partner cold-storage hubs within a 45-minute driving radius.
- If outdoor ambient temperature exceeds 35°C (heatwave alert), prioritized emergency transfer is required immediately.
