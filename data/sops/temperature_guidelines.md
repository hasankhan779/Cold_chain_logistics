# Standard Operating Procedure: Temperature Range & Compliance Guidelines

## 1. Objective
Establish standard temperature bounds for cold-chain shipments to prevent spoilage, degradation, or loss of pharmaceutical and perishable goods.

## 2. Cargo Categories and Thresholds
- **mRNA Vaccines & Biologics:** Must remain between +2°C and +8°C (Refrigerated) or below -20°C (Ultra-low frozen depending on formulation).
- **Insulin & Injectables:** Strictly +2°C to +8°C. Freezing (below 0°C) permanently denatures the protein.
- **Fresh Produce / Dairy:** 0°C to +4°C.
- **Frozen Seafood / Meat:** Strictly below -18°C.

## 3. Acceptable Tolerances
- Excursions exceeding ±2°C for longer than 45 continuous minutes require immediate operator notification and root-cause inspection of the refrigeration unit (reefer).
